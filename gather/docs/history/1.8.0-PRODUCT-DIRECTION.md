# Gather product direction — 1.8.0

Preserve two connected paths: open a profile → Gather → inspect/copy, without a project; and resume a project/scan → search → inspect → deliberately save/capture → review → export → resume.

R3 is the long-term product direction, not an instruction to add its entire roadmap to this release. 1.8.0 completes reviewed Case Start, Ephemeral/Local context, stable roles, queue/coverage, explicit associations, truthful account states, Evidence IDs, metadata-only capture, review sheets and guarded private-backup closure on the preserved continuity/capture foundation.

The destination is visible. Explicit tab assignment persists during a browser session; it is research context, not proof of discovery. Capture freezes project/scan/subject on invocation. Subjects span scans within one project and remain separate from account observations. Similar names never establish identity or a relationship.

Keep the panel focused on active work; keep deep management and export in the full workspace. Quick Lookup stays focused and usable without Case Start. Capture code loads when requested. New images require deliberate inclusion in reports. Originals are immutable, redactions are derivatives, and private backups explicitly retain originals. A hash establishes byte integrity only.

Ephemeral Case minimizes intake-derived context; it does not erase public names from saved evidence. Hide friendly labels is a scoped display control. Closure removes Gather-controlled project records after a verified private backup; it is not forensic erasure and cannot remove browser history, downloaded files or clipboard contents.

## Narrow backlog

1. **Next milestone: native Chrome/Edge acceptance of packaged 1.8.0 on a permitted runner.** Resolve demonstrated activeTab, focus, menu, lifecycle, Downloads, restore, accessibility and print issues before expanding the feature set. Use the checklist in `TESTING.md`.
2. After native acceptance, strengthen adapters from current primary platform evidence and controlled fixtures. Expand affirmative gone-state detection only with reliable platform-specific signals; ambiguous failure remains technical/unknown.
3. Validate top-level full-page capture on representative controlled pages before adding scrolling-element capture. Evaluate user-selected custom roots separately with explicit permission and revoked/disconnected-folder recovery.
4. Consider full Gather Bar, organization/workflow packs, richer review/annotation and encrypted resume capsules only after observed workflow friction justifies them. They are deferred, not partially promised here.

No passive browsing collection, cloud case storage, name-based people merging, automated relationship/identity conclusions or threat scoring. X remains search-only. Native mobile integration requires its actual source and separate scope.
